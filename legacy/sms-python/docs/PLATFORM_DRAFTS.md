**Project summary**

I will customize the existing Python and SQLite SMS reception workflow for one
business using one qualified Twilio SMS number owned by you. It handles up to five
approved FAQs in the supported business-hours, areas, services, pricing and
appointment-request topics. Unapproved or unknown questions go to your team.

Customers can submit appointment requests for your confirmation. The dashboard
supports human takeover, relevant manual replies, opt-out records and an audit
trail. Requests are not confirmed bookings, and a verified service conversation
does not create marketing permission. You receive source code, configuration and
setup instructions, offline acceptance evidence and a controlled real SMS
send/receive test after the exact test plan and required external actions are
authorized. This test is included in the agreed scope; it has not yet been run.

The portfolio sample uses fictional data and simulated delivery. It does not
establish real carrier delivery or production readiness. I do not promise leads,
revenue, uninterrupted or guaranteed delivery, unlimited messaging or carrier
registration approval.

**Deliverables**

- One business profile and one qualified, client-owned Twilio SMS number configuration.
- Up to five approved FAQ entries in the existing supported topics.
- Appointment-request collection that remains pending owner confirmation.
- Authenticated operator dashboard, takeover/manual replies, opt-out/audit and
  inbox/outbox delivery views.
- Source code, safe configuration example, dependency list, setup/operations guide,
  offline regression and core signed-in dashboard acceptance evidence.
- The authorized controlled real SMS send/receive test and its actual pass,
  failure or blocked results, when readiness prerequisites are met.

**Client requirements**

Approved business facts, timezone, hours, areas, service and pricing wording;
approved request fields and human escalation procedure; one operator and an
agreed hosting/development environment; client-owned Twilio account and suitable
SMS number with applicable registration and custom-message capability; approved
route, test participant/phone control and purpose; precise messages, segment
counts, cost caps and test/access authorizations. Configure secrets privately;
do not paste them into project requirements, chat, screenshots or source.

**Steps**

1. Confirm the fixed scope, business facts, readiness prerequisites and acceptance cases.
2. Customize the existing workflow and run offline safety/domain/mock checks.
3. Complete the signed-in dashboard walkthrough, then approve the exact real test manifest.
4. Execute only the authorized test when ready; record actual evidence and any block.
5. Deliver source/instructions and agreed test cleanup/access revocation records.

**FAQs**

- **Does it confirm a booking?** It collects requests pending your confirmation;
  it does not reserve calendar slots or verify availability.
- **Can staff reply to unknown questions?** Staff review a verified customer inquiry
  and confirm a relevant service reply within its original deadline. STOP and all
  carrier/sending restrictions still apply; no marketing enrollment is created.
- **Is real SMS testing included?** Yes, one controlled, authorized send/receive
  test for the approved account, number, route and participant is in this scope.
  A suitable account, registration, permission and budget are required; the current
  project has not yet executed it. Deployment and purchases need their own approval.
- **Who pays third-party fees?** You own the provider account/number and pay number
  rental, messaging/carrier segments, registration, hosting, storage/backups and
  applicable tax/platform fees. Application quotas are not a billing hard cap.
- **Is ongoing support included?** The one-time delivery includes operations and
  cleanup instructions. Ongoing maintenance, backup scheduling, monitoring and
  incident support require a separate agreement; no subscription is implied.

**Excluded**

LLM/real AI, voice/missed-call service, CRM, calendar integration, marketing,
multi-tenant features, number/hosting purchases, provider approval guarantees,
already deployed public hosting, legal opinions, unlimited usage and 24/7 support.

and backups, revoke temporary credentials/access and close exposure. Online
deletions do not erase old backups/provider records/disk snapshots; pseudonymous
STOP/dedup/quota safety records remain under documented responsibility.
