# Demo video script and shot list

Target: **80 seconds**, English, approximately 145 spoken words at a relaxed demonstration pace. Record the real local dashboard. Keep **Demo / Simulated** visible throughout. This is a personal project for fictional Harbor & Home Cleaning with synthetic records; no actual customer, live send, commercial deployment or revenue outcome is implied.

Before recording, reset demo data once, prepare the relevant simulated scenarios and use a clean 4:3 capture region. Mask contact digits, email strings and account identifiers in any platform-facing material. Do not mask the result, consent status or Simulated label. Do not show provider credentials, unrelated tabs or personal notifications.

## 80-second master

| Time | Actual screen/action | English narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8s | Show dashboard, persistent Demo / Simulated label and business profile. | “This is a personal, simulated SMS reception demo for a fictional cleaning business. No real messages are sent.” |
| 8–20s | Send an opening-hours enquiry in the simulator; show the answer and approved business configuration. | “Common questions use approved business information. When a price or policy is missing, the workflow asks for details or hands the conversation to staff.” |
| 20–34s | Run an appointment request and complete service type, area, preferred time and contact. Open the request card. | “An enquiry becomes an appointment request with the service, area, preferred time and contact details. It stays pending until the business confirms.” |
| 34–46s | Show no-consent contact; simulate a no-answer dial result. Show blocked outcome and zero follow-up. | “A missed call alone is not permission to text. This follow-up is blocked because consent for that purpose is missing.” |
| 46–58s | Select contact with synthetic verified-purpose consent. Simulate a no-answer result; show one simulated follow-up and evidence record. | “With recorded permission for missed-call follow-up, the same workflow produces one simulated text and saves the processing record.” |
| 58–69s | Send STOP, then an ordinary enquiry or another dial event; show opted-out and blocked routine sending. | “STOP changes the permission state and blocks ordinary messages. A new enquiry or another call does not silently restore consent.” |
| 69–80s | Take over a conversation; show paused automation, simulated staff reply and explicit Resume action. End on status panel. | “Staff can take over, reply, and explicitly resume automation. The dashboard brings requests, consent history and processing outcomes together.” |

Hold each result long enough to read. Jump cuts between prepared scenarios are acceptable if they do not imply an unperformed action. For appointment collection, show a brief accelerated sequence or prepared completed conversation and keep “awaiting confirmation” legible. If a scenario cannot be completed in the specified time, use the longer master within the user's 60–90-second range rather than hiding the workflow outcome.

## Shot checklist and screenshot captions

Capture **3–5 actual running screenshots**, preferably 4:3 for a common useful master shape. The media directory contains the final captures when produced by the integration/verification run; this document alone is not evidence that a screenshot or recording exists.

1. **Approved FAQ, visible source configuration:** “Answers use approved business facts — simulated personal demo.”
2. **Appointment request:** “Request awaiting business confirmation — no slot reserved.”
3. **Consent-gated follow-up:** “A call alone grants no consent; eligible follow-up is simulated.”
4. **Opt-out history:** “STOP blocks routine messaging and records the status change.”
5. **Human takeover:** “Operator takeover pauses automation until explicitly resumed.”

Inspect images at normal viewing size. Crop without cutting off the Demo label, relevant status, primary action or result. For Upwork, use a privacy-safe capture with contact identifiers hidden; do not treat fictional phone numbers as exempt from media content review. Guru numeric upload limits were not verified, so prepare master assets and check its editor before exporting upload variants.

## Recording status

The script and shot list are deliverables regardless of recording-tool availability. A screen recording should only be described as delivered when an actual playable file has been created and verified. If no recorder is available, deliver this script and the actual screenshots and explicitly state that video recording was not performed. A silent recording is not an English narrated demonstration; pair it with this narration script and label it honestly.
